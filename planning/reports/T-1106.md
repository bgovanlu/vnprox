## Drift-detection route for `terraform plan` (decided and documented)

**`POST /spec/import`'s empty-ops response** — importing the tracked spec's current content and
checking `len(ops) == 0` (and `len(notInSpec) == 0` for "nothing live is unmanaged"). Chosen over
`GET /findings?source=drift&check=spec_drift` (T-1102) because the latter requires a separate,
already-configured server-side pin (`POST /spec/pin`) — a continuous GitOps reconciliation
primitive, not a stateless one-shot plan; a `terraform plan` run against a cluster with nothing
pinned would see zero `spec_drift` findings regardless of actual drift, which is the wrong
"clean" signal. `POST /spec/import` needs no prerequisite beyond the caller's own tracked file.
Documented in `docs/api.md`'s new section, including why the alternative was rejected, not left
implicit.
