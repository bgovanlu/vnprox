# T-1607 · Posture score & report — completion report

## What I built

A periodically-computed network security/resilience score with **named, independently-inspectable
factors** — never an opaque single number — plus a scheduled, exportable report extending T-605's
config-doc export.

### `internal/posture` (new, pure package)
`Score(Inputs) Posture` folds five named factors into one explainable score:

| factor (`name`) | source | sub-score | weight |
|---|---|---|---|
| `spof_resilience` | T-1604 `failsim.Inventory`/`failsim.Score` | reused verbatim (100 − Σ severity weights) | 30 |
| `segmentation` | fraction of guests with an **applied** T-1602 microseg policy | `100·segmented/total` | 25 |
| `exposed_ports` | guest-scope inbound any-source ACCEPTs via `internal/fw.Resolve` | `100 − 10·count` | 20 |
| `anomaly_rate` | T-1601 `source:"baseline"` findings / guest | `100 − round(50·rate)` | 15 |
| `drift_hygiene` | `GET /drift` open findings / node | `100 − round(15·rate)` | 10 |

`Posture.Overall` is the weighted mean of the **evaluated** factors only; each `Factor` exposes
`weight`/`value`/`scorePct`/`contribution`/`evaluated`/`caveat` independently (the "never folded
opaquely" contract). `Posture.Qualified` is the single "not a clean bill of health" flag.

Segmentation and exposed-ports are computed **inside** the scorer off the live inventory graph's
resolved firewall view (not passed in), so AC3 ("only applied coverage counts") holds by
construction: an applied microseg policy leaves a marker-comment rule in PVE's re-polled config; a
merely-proposed/dry-run one leaves nothing. I added an exported `microseg.RuleCommentPrefix`
constant and pointed the two existing comment sites at it (value unchanged) so posture keys off one
shared marker rather than a copy-pasted string.

## How the score handles not-evaluated dimensions (the honesty requirement)

Two mechanisms, both flip `Posture.Qualified` and are tested directly:

1. **Excluded (unknown ⇒ not counted).** A **cold-start `anomaly_rate`** — `BaselineLearned=false`,
   no learned profiles, where "zero anomalies" means "we have never looked" — is reported
   `evaluated:false`, `scorePct:-1`, `contribution:0`, with a caveat, and is **dropped from both
   numerator and denominator** of the overall. It is never allowed to contribute a phantom perfect
   100. (`TestScore_ColdStartAnomalyNotEvaluated`: overall renormalizes over the remaining weight 85
   → 69, not 70.)
2. **Qualified ceiling (partial ⇒ counted but flagged).** When `failsim` reports `NotEvaluated`
   dimensions (quorum/ceph/tunnels absent for the deployment), `spof_resilience` still counts the
   known SPOFs but carries a caveat marking the score a **ceiling** (real posture may be lower) and
   sets `Qualified`. (`TestScore_SPOFNotEvaluatedIsQualifiedCeiling`.)

The degenerate all-unevaluated case returns an honest `overall:0`/`qualified:true`
("insufficient data"), never a default 100 (`TestScore_AllUnevaluatedIsHonestZero`). The not-evaluated
factor renders as `n/a` in the export, never a misleading number.

## Deviations from the card

- **Migration added (0027) rather than none.** The task-runner note said the card "likely needs no
  migration", but the card's own deliverables explicitly require the `posture_scores` bounded table
  and AC5 is a retention test against it. Added `0027_posture_scores.sql` and bumped `kv_test.go` to
  27 exactly as the fallback instruction specified. No other migration touched.
- **No new `[posture]` config section.** The card states defaults (daily cadence, keep-last-90-or-
  400-days) but does not require them to be operator-configurable. I used exported constants
  (`store.DefaultPostureKeepCount=90`, `DefaultPostureRetentionDays=400`, `postureDefaultInterval=24h`)
  to minimize churn/surface. Easy to promote to config later if desired.
- **`Score` takes an `Inputs` struct**, not the literal 4-positional signature in the card
  (`snapshot, findings, spofInventory, segmentationState`). Same information, plus the two honesty
  signals (`BaselineLearned`, `SPOF.NotEvaluated`) the score cannot derive itself; segmentation is
  derived internally from the snapshot rather than passed in (makes AC3 hold by construction).

## Notes for the next agent / needs-hardware-validation

- **`exposed_ports` shadowing is a documented approximation.** An any-source inbound ACCEPT is
  counted unless an earlier any-source DROP/REJECT (all-ports, or same proto/port) shadows it; a
  narrower specific-source earlier rule is not treated as shadowing the broad ACCEPT. This mirrors
  `internal/fw.Resolve`'s own documented simplification of real pve-firewall host/guest chain
  separation — the exposed-ports factor is advisory. A future refinement could consult the full
  ordered evaluation semantics; flagged here rather than silently approximated. **Needs hardware
  validation** against real pve-firewall ordering if used for anything load-bearing.
- The posture UI (a dashboard tile / trend view) is out of scope for this card — only the API +
  export + score engine are delivered here.
