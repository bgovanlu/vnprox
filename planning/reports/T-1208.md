## 3. Multi-cluster perf numbers (real, this environment)

`go test ./internal/federation/ -run '^$' -bench 'BenchmarkFederation' -benchmem -benchtime=50x`,
3× scale-lab profile, real `pve.Client`s over real `httptest` listeners, real credential sealing,
shared QEMU dev host (no dedicated CPU) — a conservative upper bound, same caveat as `docs/
performance.md` §2:

| Aggregator call (backs) | mean | allocs |
|---|---|---|
| `TopologySummary` (`GET /federation/topology`) | **~14.0 ms/op** | 1.38 MB/op, 7,716 allocs/op |
| `Search` (`GET /federation/search`) | **~11.0 ms/op** | 1.31 MB/op, 8,104 allocs/op |

Both clear the provisional p95<300ms interactive budget by ~20× at 3× the single-cluster entity
count — the aggregator fans reads out concurrently. These are aggregator-method latencies (the
dominant cost), not full HTTP round-trips.
